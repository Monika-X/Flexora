Foundation

A Vision Born in a Garage

Alex M. founded Flexora in a tiny garage in 2018. Equipped with just two barbell racks, a handful of iron plates, and a burning desire to create a training environment devoid of typical corporate gym gimmicks.

Our focus was simple: teach perfect lifting mechanics, program structured progression, and foster a tightly-knit, supportive athlete crew.

Growth

Expanding the Sanctuary

As word spread, our community outgrew the garage limits. In 2021, we migrated to our first warehouse facility. We brought in Coach Jamie to set up our recovery and yoga streams, bridging the gap between heavy lifting and joint mobility.

With over 1,000 active members, we proved that scientific training combined with genuine team support could yield life-changing results.

Evolution

The 10k Sq Ft Flagship

In 2025, we opened our current 10,000 sq ft sanctuary, integrating premium Eleiko platforms, modern spin decks, advanced saunas, cold plunges, and in-house nutritionists.

This flagship space establishes Flexora as the region's ultimate performance lifestyle brand.
