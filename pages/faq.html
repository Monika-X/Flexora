Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about memberships, facilities, and more.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday through Friday from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and Saturday-Sunday from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Members with 24/7 access can use the facility via keycard outside of staffed hours.

Do you offer free trials?

Yes, all new local residents are eligible for a 7-day free trial pass to experience all our facilities and classes. You can claim yours at the front desk with a valid local ID.

Can I pause my membership?

Absolutely. You can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per calendar year due to travel, medical reasons, or just needing a break. A nominal $5/month freeze fee applies.

Are group classes included?

Unlimited group classes are fully included in our Premium and Elite memberships. Basic memberships require a $15 drop-in fee per class.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes! We have a team of certified personal trainers specializing in everything from weight loss to powerlifting prep. You can book a free consultation through your dashboard. Training packages are sold separately from the core membership.

Is there a cancellation fee?

Our month-to-month memberships have zero cancellation fees as long as you provide a 30-day notice. Annual commitments have an early termination fee equivalent to two months of dues, unless canceled due to medical reasons or relocation (proof required).

Can I bring a guest?

Premium and Elite members receive two free guest passes per month. Basic members can bring guests for a $20 daily drop-in fee. All guests must sign a liability waiver and provide a valid ID at the front desk before entering the facility.

Do you provide towels and locker rentals?

Fresh workout and shower towels are provided complimentary to all members. Daily lockers are free to use, just bring your own lock or purchase one at the front desk. Elite members receive a complimentary assigned locker with overnight storage.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help. Reach out to us and we'll get back to you shortly.
