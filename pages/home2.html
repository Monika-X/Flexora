Form Correction

Technique Masterclass

See how coach Mike corrects common mistakes to keep you lifting safe and heavy.

Common Squat Errors

  • Valgus Knee Collapse:

    Knees caving inwards during ascent, placing massive shear force on the ACL and reducing glute activation.

  • Heel Rise & Toe Weighting:

    Shifting weight to front of foot, creating forward pitch, losing bar alignment, and overworking the patellar tendon.

Flexora Coached Alignment

  • Active Knee Screwing:

    Forcing knees outward by creating rotational pressure on the floor, locking hip sockets for powerful thrust.

  • Three-Point Balanced Footing:

    Distributing barbell weight equally over the heel, big toe, and pinky toe to maintain linear vertical bar path.

Common Bench Errors

  • Flat Back & Zero Leg Drive:

    Lying completely flat on the bench, which removes mechanical shoulder support and lowers pressing force.

  • Elbow Flaring:

    Flaring elbows out to 90 degrees at the bottom, shifting weight directly onto the delicate rotator cuffs.

Flexora Coached Alignment

  • Retracted Scapula & Leg Anchoring:

    Pinching shoulder blades tight and driving feet back to transfer leg force through your core directly into the bar.

  • Tucked Elbow Path (45-60°):

    Tucking elbows slightly on descent to recruit the lat muscles and chest fibers while keeping shoulders secure.

Common Deadlift Errors

  • Lumbar Rounding:

    Pulling with a curved lower spine, which puts severe pressure on spinal discs and limits lifting efficiency.

  • Squatting the Deadlift:

    Starting with hips set too low, which forces the shins forward and blocks a straight vertical barbell path.

Flexora Coached Alignment

  • Neutral Spine & Lat Tension:

    Locking the back straight, packing the neck, and loading lats dynamically before the bar leaves the floor.

  • High Hip Hinge Setup:

    Setting hips at optimal height to load the hamstrings and glutes while keeping the bar pressed flush against the shins.
